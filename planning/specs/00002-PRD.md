# Fight Common Version Adoption and Support Evidence

## Problem Statement

The starter must prove its Fight Common support claim through its own Composer graph and booted Yii composition.

## Solution

The complete Yii platform profile is recorded against the exact Fight Common candidate in
`evidence/framework-support/receipt-v1.json`. Symfony Messenger is the selected envelope-transport fallback, not a
claim of queue-worker delivery; stable Yii Queue remains explicitly unavailable. Version 2.0 stays non-executable
until its owning authority exists.

## Implementation Decisions

- The lockfile, actual Composer candidate identity, complete default service profile, and booted journeys are adoption evidence.
- Yii owns configuration-provider and DI composition; Fight Common remains a Composer dependency only.
- JWT encoding and decoding are fixed to HS256; algorithm selection is not a public application setting.
- Yii Mail and Yii Session are forbidden unselected dependencies: neither Composer lock installs them, and Symfony
  Mailer remains the selected mail fallback.
- No speculative 2.0 breaking changes are defined here.

## Testing Decisions

- Lowest/latest resolutions, booted journeys, receipt canonicalization, `./bin/planning-check`, and `./bin/build` prove a receipt.
- Production `--no-dev` resolution and exact-candidate receipt authority close the 1.2 adoption evidence.
- Deployment security proof uses environment-sourced HMAC/JWT inputs, lazy fail-closed resolution, and explicit
  verification-only fixtures; no deployable credential fallback is accepted.
- The certified dependency refresh regenerates the lowest lock digest and receipt after checking HS256 and the
  absence of installed Yii Mail and Yii Session packages in both dependency lanes.

## Out of Scope

- Copied source, nested applications, central builds, releases, and publication.
