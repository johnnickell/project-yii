# WF-001 released consumer-document inventory

Companion to the [research note](wf-001-released-package-contract-audit.md) and [symbol census](wf-001-public-surface.md).

All 135 Markdown files under the two installed `docs/` trees are accounted for, including relocated/transcluded component guides, engineering material and identity assets. Root README, CHANGELOG, LICENSE and Composer metadata and the AccessControl behavioral requirement are additional source anchors. Upstream planning histories and contributor tooling are not treated as current consumer APIs.

Method: enumerate paths/titles, inspect guide headings and consumer entrypoints, trace the security/composition claims used in the research note to the owning guide and source. Index inclusion is not a claim that every example, optional provider or conformance suite was executed. The static API census covers all declared symbols; this document inventory bounds the documentary search and records retired/non-product material explicitly.
